Data Analyst — Growth

You'll turn product and marketing data into decisions for a consumer fintech app.

Responsibilities
- Build dashboards in Power BI or Tableau for weekly growth reviews
- Write complex SQL against BigQuery; clean and model data with Python (Pandas)
- Design and analyse A/B tests; present findings to stakeholders
- Define KPIs with product managers and track funnel metrics

Requirements
- Strong SQL and Excel; statistics fundamentals
- Experience with Python for data analysis
- Clear communication and stakeholder management

Nice to have
- dbt, Airflow, experience in fintech
